- Scheduled hub reflection now repairs broken `[[links]]` in anchored claim blocks. A link that names no live card in its scope is repointed with a `replace_block` proposal when exactly one live same-scope card is the clear target: the successor of an accepted supersede, or the one card whose id, alias, topic or title matches after case, whitespace and hyphen normalization. Alias, heading, folder prefix and extension are kept, and code spans and opaque blocks are never touched. Links with no target, several targets or only a cross-scope target are counted under `link_no_target`, `link_ambiguous_target` and `link_cross_scope_target` and left alone. Every proposal is Tier 2 and waits for a reviewer.
- Link repair opens at most 8 new proposals per run by default (`[reflect] link_repair_max_proposals`, 0 to 32) after exact merges and near-duplicate proposals, takes turns by least recently proposed, and reports counts without card text. Reflect reports now leave out the replacement wording of each op.
