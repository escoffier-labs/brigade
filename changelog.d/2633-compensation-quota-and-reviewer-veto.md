- A pending review referral no longer counts against the pending principal or scope quota while a quorum-authorized automatic compensation replaces it in the same transaction, so the inverse applies instead of retrying a `memory_proposal_principal_quota` or `memory_proposal_scope_quota` refusal every poll. Only the referral's pending slot is discounted, and an inverse that holds instead of applying is rolled back if the principal or scope would end over its limit.
- A human rejection of the watch review referral now vetoes every automatic inverse for the same `rollback_of` through a `compensation_veto` hub marker written in the same decision. The refused inverse records `compensation_vetoed` with `compensation_result: failed`, the card bytes stay unchanged, and a later, distinct trip still compensates. A rejected manual rollback proposal does not veto. `docs/memory-care.md` records the decision.
