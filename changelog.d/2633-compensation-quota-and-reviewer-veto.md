- A pending review referral no longer counts against the pending principal or scope quota while a quorum-authorized automatic compensation replaces it in the same transaction, so the inverse applies instead of retrying a `memory_proposal_principal_quota` or `memory_proposal_scope_quota` refusal every poll.
- A human rejection of a review referral now vetoes every automatic inverse for the same `rollback_of`. The refused inverse records `compensation_vetoed` with `compensation_result: failed`, the card bytes stay unchanged, and a later, distinct trip still compensates. `docs/memory-care.md` records the decision.
