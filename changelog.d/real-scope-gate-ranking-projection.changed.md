- Memory automatic apply can now pass on a real scope for evidence appends and block anchoring, because the gate compares a ranking-only projection instead of raw card bytes. Evidence refs and anchor ids are no longer searchable (they are still stored and shown), so the hub rebuilds its search index on the next start and older retrieval evaluation records stop binding until the next evaluation. A trusted card that links to or is named by another card in its scope now goes to review instead of auto-applying.
- The real-scope gate now also holds a target that is an end of a trusted lineage edge in the published index, a neighbour that names the target through escaped or concatenated ids, and an unreviewed target whose applier is not its writer. A blank metadata line no longer makes every evidence append on a card look like a ranking change, and a proposal held for lineage review is proposed again by reflection after seven days.
