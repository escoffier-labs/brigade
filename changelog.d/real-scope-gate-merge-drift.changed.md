- The memory retrieval gate now has a bounded-drift rule for exact-duplicate merges on a real scope. The retired card must be newer than the keeper, trust and `automated` must match, the keeper must keep every search token and every literal line of the retired card, no live card may link to the retired id in any spelling, and the keeper may change only by the aliases and evidence the merge writes. The retired card's matches move to the keeper, while other cards' scores shift with the scope-wide statistics and near-ties can reorder. Each failed check holds with its own `retrieval_eval_merge_*` code. Merges still wait for review because the one-card blast cap, evidence floors and trust gate hold them first.
