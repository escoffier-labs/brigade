- Scheduled hub reflection now also finds near-duplicate cards. Cards whose claim bodies reach a word 5-gram Jaccard threshold (default 0.9, set from 0.5 to 1.0 with `[reflect] near_duplicate_threshold` in `memory-care.toml`) get `supersede_card` proposals that keep the most recently revised card. These proposals are Tier 2 and always wait for a reviewer.
- A full hub proposal quota no longer fails scheduled reflection or counts toward its pause. The run stops proposing and counts the unsent submissions as `proposal_quota`. A duplicate pending refusal now records the existing proposal instead of storing a refusal.
